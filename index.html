Frequently Asked Questions

More answers in the ACEB FAQ.

Which cryptocurrencies can I pay with?

Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Litecoin, USDC, EURC, BNB, XRP, Polygon, Dogecoin and Tron. Binance Pay and WalletConnect are supported too, and registered customers can pay by SEPA bank transfer.

Do I need an account?

No. A crypto checkout needs only the email address the gift card is sent to. An account is optional: it is needed for SEPA bank transfer and to earn cashback.

How fast is delivery?

Gift cards are sent by email as soon as the payment is confirmed. Most crypto payments confirm within a few minutes.

Where can a gift card be used?

Each gift card is region-specific. Before buying, check that the card matches the country where it will be redeemed.

Can an AI assistant shop for me?

Yes. ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants can search the catalog and prepare a cart through the ACEB MCP server. You always complete the payment yourself.

Can I get a refund?

Gift card codes are digital and delivered instantly, so a delivered gift card cannot be returned or refunded. If a code does not work, contact support@aceb.com. See the return policy.
