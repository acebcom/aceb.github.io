# acebcom.github.io

Source of [acebcom.github.io](https://acebcom.github.io) — the GitHub Pages site of [ACEB.com](https://aceb.com), an online shop for digital gift cards paid with cryptocurrency.

ACEB lets you spend Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, USDC and other cryptocurrencies on thousands of gift cards from global retail, gaming, streaming, travel and food brands, delivered by email.
